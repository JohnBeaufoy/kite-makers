        Aircraft come from your hangar. Choose how many of each to buy. Prices are for the year of the battle, and an engine past its prime costs more to keep in service.

        Take one carrier to sea on her own, with no enemy and no escorts, and work up her flying: range aircraft on deck, launch them, send them out and land them again. Her deck, lifts, barrier, catapults and arrester gear, the wind over the deck and the roll and pitch of the sea all set how fast it goes.

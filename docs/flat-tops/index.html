        Air group

        Aircraft come from your hangar. Choose how many of each to buy. Prices are for the year of the battle, and an engine past its prime costs more to keep in service.

        Take one carrier to sea on her own, with no escorts, and work up her flying: range aircraft on deck, launch them, send them out and land them again. Her deck, lifts, barrier, catapults and arrester gear, the wind over the deck and the roll and pitch of the sea all set how fast it goes. Anchor target ships anywhere on the chart and send squadrons from the Air group to attack them. With Advanced trials on in Settings, take a whole task force out against enemy task forces that hunt for you.

          Rival fleets

          One rival fleet a year. Each year sets how many tons of carriers you may take and how much you may spend on aircraft. Beating a rival moves the calendar on and releases that year's parts.

          Air group

          Click aircraft to pick them, or a type's name to pick all of that type in that column. Make the picked aircraft a squadron. Then tick squadrons and send them on a sortie, or to protect the carrier.
